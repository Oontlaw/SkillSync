Unaddressed-after-return rate between named pairs, corrected against each member's own baseline

Interpersonal data — admin tier only. This view shows how often one member's directed pings go unaddressed by another specific member, measured as a deviation from the pinger's own baseline (not an absolute "rate of ignoring" — no intent is inferred; a ping counts as unaddressed only when the pingee returned to activity and still did not respond within the window). Deliberately separate from the interaction graph.
